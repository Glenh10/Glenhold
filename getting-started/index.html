What to give your bookkeeper

A simpler handoff starts with the right records.

Book a Free Call

What should you give a new bookkeeper?

Start with a complete list of your business accounts, statements for the periods being worked on, and access to the existing bookkeeping records. A clear picture of what is available helps your bookkeeper identify gaps and define the work.

This checklist is for small business owners preparing for ongoing bookkeeping or a cleanup project. Not every item will apply. Discuss how to share records with Jack before sending sensitive documents.

Your bookkeeping handoff checklist

  1. Business and contact details. The name your business uses, your contact information, and who can answer questions about transactions.
  2. A complete account list. Include business checking, savings, credit cards, loans, and payment processors. Include closed accounts and any accounts replaced during the year.
  3. Bank and credit card statements. Gather every statement for the relevant period, including pages showing opening and closing balances. Transaction exports are useful alongside statements.
  4. Existing accounting records. Identify your QuickBooks Online file, the latest completed month, prior financial statements, and any known issues.
  5. Loan and financing records. Provide statements or schedules that distinguish principal, interest, and outstanding balances where applicable.
  6. Payroll reports. If payroll entries need to be recorded or reviewed, identify the payroll provider and the reports available.
  7. Owner transactions. Flag money you contributed or withdrew and business expenses paid personally, so their treatment can be discussed.
  8. Deadlines and professional contacts. Tell Jack about tax-preparer requests, financing deadlines, and who will receive the completed reports.

Three details that prevent an incomplete handoff

Include both sides of bank transfers

If money moved between accounts, make sure the records for both accounts are available. A withdrawal in one account alone does not explain the destination.

Identify the last reconciled month

The last month with transactions entered may be different from the last month reconciled to a statement. Share both if you know them; if you do not, say so during the assessment.

Call out what you cannot find

A missing statement or an uncertain opening balance is useful information. Keep a short list of missing records so the work can be planned around the gaps.

What happens after the handoff?

Jack can discuss your priorities, assess the scope, and explain whether the next step is a cleanup project, regular monthly support, or QuickBooks consulting. Request a free introductory call before gathering documents you may not need.

About Jack

I live here, I do the books myself, and you work with me.

Book a Free Call

As a Simi Valley local, I am proud to raise my family in this beautiful community. I've always had a natural affinity for math, and a college professor showed me it was a perfect fit for accounting.

After earning my Accounting degree, I began my career at a Big 4 firm, handling high-level accounting and bookkeeping work for multi-million dollar enterprises and auditing Fortune 500 financial services companies.

I founded Glenhold to bring that high-level expertise and insight to the local businesses that serve as the backbone of our country.

When I'm not working, I enjoy fly fishing, going on walks, and attending Mass with my wife and two children. I believe that professional excellence is rooted in personal character; as I often say, integrity is not something you can choose on a whim—it must be practiced daily over a lifetime.

Monthly bookkeeping for small businesses across industries

Glenhold Bookkeeping provides ongoing bookkeeping for small businesses in Simi Valley and nearby Ventura County and Los Angeles County communities. Work directly with Jack, a former Big 4 accountant and Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor, to keep transactions organized and financial reports understandable.

You do not need to fit a particular industry to work with Glenhold. The scope is built around your accounts, transaction volume, reporting needs, and the way your business operates.

What is included?

  • Transaction categorization: income and expenses organized using a chart of accounts suited to your business.
  • Bank and credit card reconciliation: balances checked against statements so missing entries, duplicates, and unmatched transfers can be addressed.
  • Monthly financial statements: a profit and loss statement and balance sheet to help you understand performance and balances.
  • Communication between closes: email support for questions and the information needed to finish your books.

The monthly service targets a P&L and balance sheet by the 15th. Timely statements and answers to bookkeeping questions help keep that schedule on track.

How getting started works

  1. Tell Jack about your business. Discuss the accounts you use, your current bookkeeping, and what you need from your reports.
  2. Agree on scope and pricing. Identify whether you need ongoing support, cleanup first, or both.
  3. Set up access and a monthly routine. Establish how records and bookkeeping questions will be handled.

If your books are behind, start with cleanup and catch-up bookkeeping. If you want to keep doing the work yourself, consider QuickBooks Online setup and training.

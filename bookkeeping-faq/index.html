Monthly bookkeeping

What does monthly bookkeeping include?

All monthly plans include expense categorization, monthly bank and credit card reconciliation, monthly financial statements, and clean books for tax season. Core adds a quarterly profit and expense summary, a year-to-date performance comparison, and priority support. Strategic adds custom financial reports, accrual accounting, payroll journal entries, loan tracking, and a monthly call to walk through your numbers.

When are monthly financial statements ready?

Monthly bookkeeping targets a profit and loss and a balance sheet by the 15th of the following month. That timing depends on statements and answers arriving in time to reconcile. If a statement is late, the close can slip. All three monthly plans include these statements.

What is a profit and loss statement?

A profit and loss statement shows income, expenses, and the profit or loss for a period, usually a month or a year. It does not, by itself, show how much cash is in the bank. Glenhold's monthly plans include a profit and loss, with a target of the 15th for the prior month.

What is a balance sheet?

A balance sheet shows what the business owns, what it owes, and the owner's equity on a given date. Bank balances, credit cards, loans, and owner draws affect it. Glenhold's monthly plans include a balance sheet along with the profit and loss.

What is a bank reconciliation?

A bank or credit card reconciliation checks the balance in the books against the statement. Statement lines should match book entries, including fees, interest, and transfers. When it ties, that account can be closed for the month. When it does not, something is missing, duplicated, or in the wrong account. Every Glenhold monthly plan includes this work.

Does Glenhold process payroll?

No. Glenhold does not run payroll and does not file payroll tax returns. Strategic bookkeeping includes payroll journal entries so totals from your payroll provider can be recorded in the books. Running payroll stays with your payroll company.

Can I switch between Essential, Core, and Strategic?

Yes. The plans are levels of support, and you can adjust as you grow. A move is a scope change, usually because volume, accounts, or reporting needs changed. Ask before the next close rather than assuming a mid-month switch.

Cleanup and catch-up

Can you help if my books are months or years behind?

Yes. Cleanup and catch-up services can cover unfinished periods, transaction classification, imports from statements and exports, reconciliation, and a clean handoff to your CPA. The scope and timeline depend on the records available and the work required.

What is the difference between bookkeeping cleanup and catch-up?

Cleanup means correcting books that already exist: duplicates, wrong categories, and balances that do not match the bank. Catch-up means recording periods that were never finished. Many projects are both. Glenhold does either, for periods you still have records for, and can hand the result to your CPA.

What records do you need for catch-up bookkeeping?

Start with the months that need work, every business bank and credit card account including accounts you closed, and statements that show opening and closing balances. Add loan statements, payroll reports, and a note of money you put in or took out if those apply. If you changed banks, include both accounts. Ask how to share files before you send sensitive documents.

How long does catch-up bookkeeping take?

It depends on the size of the backlog, missing statements, and how quickly open questions get answered. A short gap with complete statements moves faster than several years with closed accounts and no opening balance. Tell Jack about any tax or lender deadline on the first call. The site does not promise a fixed number of days.

Will catch-up bookkeeping make my books ready for a CPA?

That is the point of a cleanup handoff. You get classified transactions, reconciled accounts, and financial statements, plus a note of anything the records could not support. Your CPA uses that package for the return. Glenhold does not file the tax return.

Can bookkeeping cleanup prepare numbers a lender can review?

Lenders often ask for a profit and loss, a balance sheet, and recent bank statements. Glenhold can catch up and reconcile the books and produce those financial statements. That is not a loan application and it is not an approval. The lender may still ask your CPA for tax returns.

What is a chart of accounts?

The chart of accounts is the list of categories in the books: income, expenses, assets, liabilities, and equity. A useful chart matches how the business operates. Too many overlapping accounts make a profit and loss hard to read. Too few hide what you wanted to see. Glenhold builds or revises the chart during setup, cleanup, or monthly work.

Bookkeeper and CPA

What is the difference between a bookkeeper and a CPA?

A bookkeeper records transactions, reconciles bank and credit card accounts, and prepares financial statements. A CPA is a licensed accountant who can prepare and sign tax returns, give tax advice, and, when engaged for it, perform audits or reviews. Glenhold Bookkeeping does the bookkeeping. Tax filing stays with your CPA or other tax preparer.

Do I need a bookkeeper if I already have a CPA?

Often, yes. A CPA closing a year of unsorted transactions is doing bookkeeping under time pressure. A bookkeeper keeps the months current so the CPA can prepare the return from reconciled statements. Glenhold's work is built for that handoff. Hiring Glenhold does not replace your tax preparer.

Does bookkeeping include filing my tax return?

The listed services focus on bookkeeping and preparing organized records for tax season. Tax return preparation and filing are separate from the bookkeeping services described here; coordinate those with your CPA or tax preparer.

What does clean books for tax season mean?

It means the accounts are categorized, bank and card accounts are reconciled, and the profit and loss and balance sheet are ready to hand to your tax preparer. It does not mean the tax return has been prepared or filed. Your CPA still reviews the books and files the return.

Can a bookkeeper give tax advice?

Glenhold does not offer tax advice or tax return preparation. Questions about deductions, entity choice, and what a payment should be called for tax purposes belong with your CPA or other tax preparer. Jack will record payments in the account you and your preparer agree on rather than guess at a tax position.

California basics

What is the California $800 LLC franchise tax?

Most California limited liability companies owe an annual minimum franchise tax of $800 to the Franchise Tax Board, including years with little or no profit. A temporary waiver of that first-year tax applied only to LLCs that organized or registered in 2021, 2022, or 2023. Glenhold does not file the franchise-tax return. Confirm whether the $800 applies to your entity, and when it is due, with your CPA or the Franchise Tax Board.

Does every small business in California owe the $800 franchise tax?

No. The $800 minimum franchise tax applies to California LLCs and to corporations, not to a sole proprietor who has not formed one of those entities. A sole proprietor still has income tax and, if they sell taxable goods, sales tax to think about. Whether to form an LLC is a legal and tax decision. Glenhold can keep books either way.

What is the California LLC fee on total income?

California LLCs can owe an LLC fee based on total income, separate from the $800 minimum franchise tax. The fee uses income tiers set in state law and, for many years, has started once total income reaches $250,000. Confirm the current tiers and the income definition with your CPA or the Franchise Tax Board. The books matter because the fee follows total income, not only profit.

How should California sales tax show up in the books?

Sales tax you collect is not revenue. It is a liability until it is paid to the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration. The sale itself is income. Tax collected sits in a sales-tax payable account, and the payment reduces that account. Glenhold can record those amounts from your sales reports. Filing the return is separate.

What is the California sales tax rate?

California's statewide base sales and use tax rate is 7.25 percent. District taxes added by cities and counties make the rate you charge higher in many places, and those add-ons change. Do not copy a rate from a neighboring city. Look up the current rate for the place of sale on the CDTFA website. Glenhold records the tax you collect. This site does not assign a rate for your business.

Does Glenhold file California sales tax returns?

No. The listed services are bookkeeping, cleanup, QuickBooks Online setup and training, and the reports that come with the monthly plans. They do not include filing sales and use tax returns. The books can show taxable sales, tax collected, and tax paid so the person who files has a trail.

Does Glenhold file the California franchise tax?

No. Preparing and filing the Franchise Tax Board return, including the LLC return, is tax work for your CPA or other tax preparer. Glenhold keeps the bookkeeping those forms draw from: income, expenses, and reconciled balances. Your preparer confirms which form applies.

How should estimated tax payments be recorded?

When estimated federal or California taxes are paid from a business account, the payment should be recorded. Depending on the entity it may be a draw, a distribution, or a tax payment, not an operating expense like rent. The category is a question for your CPA. Glenhold records the payment in the account you and your CPA agree on.

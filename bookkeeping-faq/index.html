Bookkeeping services, pricing, and local coverage

Learn how Glenhold works before choosing your next step. For the full plan comparison, see monthly pricing. For city coverage, visit our service areas.

How much does Glenhold bookkeeping cost?

Monthly bookkeeping plans start at $349 for Essential, $599 for Core, and $1,499 for Strategic. These are starting prices; the quote depends on the volume and complexity of the work. Cleanup, catch-up, consulting, and training are scoped separately.

What does monthly bookkeeping include?

All monthly plans include expense categorization, bank and credit card reconciliation, monthly financial statements, and clean books for tax season. Core adds invoice and bill tracking, a quarterly performance summary, and priority support. Strategic adds custom reporting and a monthly video call, among other services.

Do you only work with certain industries?

No. Glenhold works with small businesses across industries. The scope is based on your accounts, bookkeeping needs, and reporting goals rather than a single industry specialty.

Where does Glenhold provide bookkeeping services?

Glenhold is based in Simi Valley and serves surrounding Ventura County and Los Angeles County communities, including Moorpark, Thousand Oaks, Westlake Village, Camarillo, the San Fernando Valley, and Santa Clarita. The local focus is roughly an hour from Simi Valley, depending on traffic and location.

Can you help if my books are months or years behind?

Yes. Cleanup and catch-up services can cover unfinished periods, transaction classification, imports from statements and exports, reconciliation, and a clean handoff to your CPA. The scope and timeline depend on the records available and the work required.

Can you help with QuickBooks Online?

Yes. Glenhold offers QuickBooks Online setup, chart of accounts organization, consulting, training, and accuracy check-ins. Jack is a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor. You can discuss full monthly bookkeeping if you later want ongoing support.

Will I work directly with the owner?

Yes. You work directly with Jack, the founder of Glenhold Bookkeeping, a Simi Valley local and former Big 4 accountant.

Does bookkeeping include filing my tax return?

The listed services focus on bookkeeping and preparing organized records for tax season. Tax return preparation and filing are separate from the bookkeeping services described here; coordinate those with your CPA or tax preparer.

What should I prepare for a bookkeeping assessment?

Start with a list of your business bank and credit card accounts, the periods you need help with, the most recent completed reports, and any deadlines. Mention closed accounts, missing statements, and known issues. Ask how to share records before sending sensitive documents.

How do I get started?

Book a free introductory call or send a message through the website. Explain your business, your current bookkeeping situation, and the help you need. Monthly service includes a first-month guarantee: love your first month of books, or it is free.

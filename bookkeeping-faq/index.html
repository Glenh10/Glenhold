Monthly bookkeeping

What does monthly bookkeeping include?

Every monthly plan includes expense categorization, bank and credit card reconciliation, monthly financial statements, and clean books for tax season. Core adds a quarterly summary, a year-to-date comparison, and priority support. Strategic adds custom reports, accrual accounting, payroll journal entries, loan tracking, and a monthly call.

When are monthly financial statements ready?

I aim for your profit and loss and balance sheet by the 15th of the next month. That depends on statements and answers arriving in time. If a statement is late, the close can slip. All three plans include these statements.

What is a profit and loss statement?

It shows income, expenses, and profit for a period, usually a month or a year. It does not, by itself, show the cash in the bank. I include a profit and loss with the monthly close, aimed at the 15th.

What is a balance sheet?

It's a snapshot of what the business owns, what it owes, and what's left for you. Bank balances, cards, loans, and draws all show up there. I send one with the profit and loss each month.

What is a bank reconciliation?

I match the books to the bank or card statement. Fees, interest, and transfers have to be in there too. When it ties, that account is closed for the month. When it doesn't, something is missing, duplicated, or in the wrong place. Every monthly plan includes this.

Does Glenhold process payroll?

I don't run payroll. If you already use a payroll company, the Strategic plan includes recording those payroll journal entries in your books.

Can I switch between Essential, Core, and Strategic?

Yes. Tell me before the next close. People usually move because the volume, the accounts, or the reports they want have changed.

Cleanup and catch-up

Can you help if my books are months or years behind?

Yes. I'll classify the transactions, pull in what we can from statements and exports, reconcile the accounts, and hand your CPA a clean package. How long it takes depends on the records you have.

What is the difference between bookkeeping cleanup and catch-up?

Cleanup means the books exist and they're wrong: duplicates, bad categories, balances that don't match the bank. Catch-up means months that were never entered. A lot of projects are both. I'll do either, and I'll hand the result to your CPA.

What records do you need for catch-up bookkeeping?

The months you want finished, every business bank and card account, including ones you closed, and statements that show the opening and closing balances. Loans, payroll reports, and money you put in or took out help too. If you changed banks, I need both. Ask me how to send files before you email anything sensitive.

How long does catch-up bookkeeping take?

It depends on the backlog, what's missing, and how fast we can answer open questions. A short gap with full statements moves faster than years with no opening balance. Tell me the tax or lender date on the first call so I can plan around it.

Will catch-up bookkeeping make my books ready for a CPA?

That's the goal. You get classified transactions, reconciled accounts, financial statements, and a note on anything the records couldn't support. Your CPA files the return. I don't.

Can bookkeeping cleanup prepare numbers a lender can review?

Lenders usually want a profit and loss, a balance sheet, and recent bank statements. I can catch the books up and give you those. I don't fill out the loan application, and I can't promise an approval. They may still ask your CPA for the tax returns.

What is a chart of accounts?

It's the list of categories in your books: income, expenses, assets, liabilities, and equity. A good chart matches how you operate. Too many accounts make the profit and loss noisy. Too few hide what you wanted to see. I build or clean that list during setup, catch-up, or monthly work.

Bookkeeper and CPA

What is the difference between a bookkeeper and a CPA?

I record the transactions, reconcile the accounts, and prepare the financial statements. A CPA is licensed to prepare and sign tax returns, give tax advice, and, when hired for it, do audits or reviews. I do the books. Your CPA files the return.

Do I need a bookkeeper if I already have a CPA?

Usually, yes. If your CPA is sorting a year of transactions in March, they're doing bookkeeping under pressure. I keep the months current so they can file from reconciled statements. Hiring me doesn't replace your tax preparer.

Does bookkeeping include filing my tax return?

I don't prepare tax returns, but I'll hand your CPA clean, tax-ready books.

What does clean books for tax season mean?

The accounts are categorized, the bank and cards are reconciled, and the profit and loss and balance sheet are ready for your tax preparer. Your CPA still reviews them and files the return.

Can a bookkeeper give tax advice?

I don't give tax advice or prepare the return. Questions about deductions and what a payment should be called belong with your CPA. I'll record the payment in the account the two of you agree on.

Why does a small-business bookkeeper mention Big 4 experience?

I started at a Big 4 firm, on large company books and on audits of Fortune 500 financial services companies. For you that means a chart of accounts that fits, reconciliations that tie to the bank, and reports you can read. I don't offer audits.

California basics

What is the California $800 LLC franchise tax?

Most California LLCs owe an annual minimum franchise tax of $800 to the Franchise Tax Board, even in a slow year. A first-year waiver applied only to LLCs that registered in 2021, 2022, or 2023. I don't file that return. Your CPA or the Franchise Tax Board can confirm what you owe and when.

Does every small business in California owe the $800 franchise tax?

No. The $800 minimum is for California LLCs and corporations, not for a sole proprietor who hasn't formed one. You can still owe income tax, and sales tax if you sell taxable goods. Whether to form an LLC is a question for your CPA or an attorney. I can keep the books either way.

What is the California LLC fee on total income?

California LLCs can also owe a fee based on total income, separate from the $800 minimum. For many years that fee has started once total income reaches $250,000. Confirm the current tiers with your CPA or the Franchise Tax Board. Clean books matter because the fee follows total income, not only profit.

How should California sales tax show up in the books?

Sales tax you collect is not your money. I record the sale as income and the tax as a liability until it's paid to the CDTFA. I can do that from your sales reports. Your preparer, or whoever you use, files the return.

What is the California sales tax rate?

The statewide base rate is 7.25 percent. Cities and counties add district taxes, and those change. Don't copy the rate from the next city over. Look up the current rate for where you sell on the CDTFA site. I'll record the tax you actually collect.

Does Glenhold file California sales tax returns?

I don't file the sales tax return. I keep taxable sales, tax collected, and tax paid clear in the books so the person who files has a trail.

Does Glenhold file the California franchise tax?

I don't file it. I keep the income, expenses, and reconciled balances your CPA uses for the Franchise Tax Board return.

How should estimated tax payments be recorded?

If you pay estimates from the business account, I record the payment. Depending on your entity it may be a draw, a distribution, or a tax payment, not an expense like rent. Your CPA picks the category. I'll book it where you both want it.

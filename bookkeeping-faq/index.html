Your bookkeeping questions, answered

What the work costs, what a month includes, and how to start.

Book a Free Call

Before the first call

Short answers about price, the monthly close, catch-up, QuickBooks Online, and where I work. If your question is more specific, book a call or send a note.

How much does monthly bookkeeping cost?

Essential is $349 a month to start, Core is $599, and Strategic is $1,499. Your price depends on transaction volume, accounts, and reporting. Catch-up and training are priced on their own.

What is included in monthly bookkeeping?

I categorize transactions, reconcile your bank and credit cards, and send a profit and loss and a balance sheet. Core adds a quarterly summary, a year-to-date comparison, and priority support. Strategic adds custom reports and a monthly call.

Do you work with my type of business?

I work with local small businesses of every kind. Tell me how you get paid, which accounts you use, and what you want the reports to show.

Can you help if my books are behind?

Yes. I record the missing months, reconcile the accounts, and send you reports. I price that from the records you have, and you can move to a monthly close after.

Can you help me with QuickBooks Online?

Yes. I am a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor. I set up the file, organize the chart of accounts, and train you on the work you want to keep.

Who will I work with?

You work with me. I live in Simi Valley. I have been in accounting since 2021, including audit work at PwC, and I am a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor.

When will I get my reports?

I aim for the 15th of the following month. If a statement or a question is still outstanding, I tell you what is missing and send the reports as soon as I have what I need.

Can you work with my tax preparer?

Yes. I hand your CPA clean, reconciled books. They file the return.

Where do you work?

I am in Simi Valley, and I work with owners throughout Ventura County and nearby Los Angeles communities, including Moorpark, Thousand Oaks, Camarillo, Oxnard, Ventura, Santa Clarita, and the towns around them. If you are nearby, I would be glad to talk.

What does the first-month guarantee cover?

Love your first month of books, or it is free. That covers monthly bookkeeping. Catch-up and QuickBooks training are priced on their own.

What is the difference between the three plans?

Essential keeps the month current. Core adds a quarterly summary and a year-to-date comparison. Strategic adds custom reports, accrual, payroll journal entries, loan tracking, and a monthly call.

What should I send before the first call?

An account list, the months you care about, and any deadline. Leave bank logins and statements off the first form. I will tell you how to share files.

How do you record California sales tax?

I record the sale as income and the tax you collected as an amount to pay over. The statewide base rate is 7.25 percent, and your city rate can be higher. Your preparer files the return.

Do you run payroll?

Your payroll company runs payroll. On the Strategic plan I record those payroll journal entries in the books.

Can you help a brand-new business?

Yes. You can start with QuickBooks Online setup, or with monthly bookkeeping once there is an account to reconcile.

What is the California $800 LLC tax?

Most California LLCs owe an $800 minimum franchise tax each year, including quiet years. A first-year waiver covered only registrations in 2021, 2022, and 2023. Your CPA files it. I keep the books.

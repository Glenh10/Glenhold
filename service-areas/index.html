Bookkeeping around Simi Valley

One firm. A region of cities. No claim of an office in each of them.

Book a Free Call

Glenhold Bookkeeping is a Simi Valley-based bookkeeping firm owned by Jack Hawley and operated by Glenhold Advisory LLC. Call (805) 419-3451 or email jack@glenhold.com. Hours are Monday through Saturday, 6am to 6pm Pacific. No street address is published. Day-to-day bookkeeping is remote, with in-person meetings by arrangement in the service area.

About an hour from Simi Valley, traffic permitting

Glenhold Bookkeeping is based in Simi Valley, California. There is no published street address and no office in each city. Day-to-day bookkeeping is remote. In-person meetings are by arrangement when they help. The focus is roughly an hour's drive. Some cities on this list, including Ojai, Malibu, Glendale, and parts of the east Valley, often take longer. Those pages say so.

Oak Park, Winnetka, and Tarzana do not have their own pages. They sit between cities that do, and they are part of the same service area. Name the neighborhood when you write.

Simi Valley and the east county

Simi Valley

Home base. Remote books, and the easiest place to meet in person. Every kind of small business along the 118.

Moorpark

The next city north of Simi, along the 118 and the 23. Often a short drive for a meeting.

Conejo Valley and the 101

Thousand Oaks

Conejo Valley's main city, along the 101 and the 23. A straightforward drive from Simi when a meeting helps.

Newbury Park

Western Thousand Oaks, toward Camarillo. Part of the city, with its own drive from Simi via the 101.

Westlake Village

A small Los Angeles County city on the Ventura line, with office parks along the 101. Same prices as Simi Valley.

Agoura Hills

Between Westlake Village and Calabasas on the 101, with Kanan Road toward Malibu.

Calabasas

The 101, The Commons, and Malibu Canyon. Small-business plans, not a Calabasas premium.

Camarillo

Between Newbury Park and Oxnard on the 101. Old Town, the outlets, the airport, and the plain.

Coast, Oxnard plain, and Highway 126

Oxnard

Ventura County's largest city. Harbor, fields, and Boulevard businesses are all in scope. The drive argues for a remote close.

Ventura

The county seat, officially San Buenaventura. Downtown, the harbor, and the 101. Often about an hour from Simi.

Port Hueneme

A separate beach city south of Oxnard, beside the naval base. Local businesses, not a government-contracting claim.

Santa Paula

Highway 126 and Main Street, between Ventura and Fillmore. Citrus country, every kind of small business.

Fillmore

East of Santa Paula on Highway 126. A smaller downtown, a longer drive, the same remote bookkeeping.

Ojai

Highway 33, no freeway. Often more than an hour from Simi. Remote books are the practical fit.

Santa Clarita

Santa Clarita

Valencia, Newhall, Saugus, and Canyon Country in one city. Usually the 118 to the 5 from Simi.

San Fernando Valley along the 118

Chatsworth

Just east of Simi on the 118. Business parks and neighborhood commerce. Often a short meeting drive.

Porter Ranch

East of Chatsworth on the 118. Town Center retail, clinics, and local services. A short trip from Simi.

Northridge

CSUN and the Reseda, Nordhoff, and Devonshire corridors. Any small business, not a campus niche.

Granada Hills

Between Porter Ranch and Northridge. Balboa and Chatsworth Street. Neighborhood businesses of every kind.

Southwest and central San Fernando Valley

Woodland Hills

Warner Center and Ventura Boulevard in the southwest Valley. Operating books, not a trust-account practice.

Canoga Park

Sherman Way and Topanga. Owner-run shops and trades. Catch-up is quoted from the backlog, not the monthly start.

Reseda

Reseda Boulevard and Sherman Way. Independent businesses of every kind, scoped by the books.

Encino

Ventura Boulevard professional offices and the neighborhood beside the Sepulveda Basin. Operating books only.

Van Nuys

Central Valley: Van Nuys Boulevard, the civic center, and the airport. Not the same page as Sherman Oaks.

Southeast Valley and nearby cities

Sherman Oaks

Ventura Boulevard and Fashion Square. Often close to an hour from Simi. The monthly call does not have to be in person.

Studio City

Ventura Boulevard east of Sherman Oaks. Small businesses, not a studio accounting shop. Often an hour-plus from Simi.

North Hollywood

NoHo Arts District and the wider east Valley grid. Often an hour or more from Simi. Not a nonprofit-tax practice.

Burbank

Its own city east of the Valley. Downtown, Magnolia Park, and the airport. Small business books, not studio payroll.

Glendale

East of Burbank. Often past an hour from Simi. On the list because the work is remote, not because the drive is short.

Malibu

Malibu

PCH and the canyons. The drive is unpredictable and often over an hour. Remote bookkeeping is the actual service.

Bookkeeping for Westlake Village businesses

Westlake Village bookkeeping starts with a clear month: I categorize your transactions, reconcile the accounts, and send a profit and loss and a balance sheet, usually by the 15th. The city sits in Los Angeles County, on the edge of Thousand Oaks. Plans start at $349. You work with me.

Book a Free Call

Westlake Village is a city of its own, in Los Angeles County, even though daily life runs straight into Thousand Oaks. I treat it that way in the books. Your address, your sales-tax district, and the way you describe the business all stay with Westlake Village. The monthly work is still the work: categorize, reconcile, and send statements you can read.

The businesses I hear from here are often professional offices, consultancies, medical practices, and owner-run firms with a small staff. The transactions are rents, professional income, payroll transfers from a payroll company, owner draws, and a card that mixes software with the occasional personal charge. Naming those cleanly is what makes the profit and loss trustworthy.

On the first call, tell me whether the books are current. A current file becomes a monthly plan. An open stretch of months becomes a catch-up quote, priced from the records, and the monthly plan starts when that stretch is finished. I live in Simi Valley and I am glad to meet in the Conejo when a walkthrough on paper helps. Most questions are handled when I send the reports.

I've been in accounting since 2021, including audit work at PwC, and I'm a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor. I bring that care to Westlake Village owners who want one person on the close.

California sales tax, when you collect it, sits on the balance sheet as a liability. The statewide base rate is 7.25 percent, and the district rate for your Westlake Village location can be higher. I look it up. Your preparer files the return. The same split holds for the LLC's $800 franchise tax: I keep the books, and your CPA files the form.

What the monthly work looks like here

  1. Confirm the city and the accounts. Westlake Village, Los Angeles County. Checking, savings, cards, and any processor.
  2. Separate payroll and owner activity. Your payroll company runs payroll. I record the journal entries on the Strategic plan, and I label draws and contributions on every plan.
  3. Categorize the operating month. Fees, rent, software, insurance, and the costs that belong to the work.
  4. Reconcile. Bank and cards tie to QuickBooks Online.
  5. Deliver the reports. Profit and loss and balance sheet by the 15th, with the extra views your plan includes.

A short list before the first close

  • The QuickBooks Online file, if you have one
  • Statements or bank feeds for the months in scope
  • Payroll reports, if staff are paid through a payroll company
  • The question you most want the profit and loss to answer

Common questions

Is Westlake Village part of Thousand Oaks for the books?

It is its own city, in Los Angeles County. I keep a page for it so the records and the conversation match the city you are actually in.

I run a small professional office here. Which plan fits?

Essential starts at $349 a month when the accounts are straightforward. Core, at $599, adds a quarterly summary and a year-to-date comparison.

Can you work with the CPA I already have?

Yes. I hand your CPA clean, reconciled books. They file the return.

What if QuickBooks Online is only half set up?

Then setup is the first project. I build the chart of accounts and train you on the parts you want to keep.

When do the reports arrive?

A Thousand Oaks close, step by step

  1. Name the location and the accounts. Boulevard office, Newbury Park shop, or a practice that sees people in both. The accounts matter more than the suite number.
  2. Match deposits to the report that explains them. Processor batches, insurance remits, or invoices. The bank line is the total. The report is the detail.
  3. Code the month in your language. Patient revenue, supplies, professional fees, rent, payroll transfers, owner draws.
  4. Reconcile bank and cards. Each balance ties out. Leftover items stay on a list until you and I name them.
  5. Send the 15th package. Profit and loss and balance sheet. Core adds the quarterly and year-to-date view. Strategic adds the monthly call.

Useful before I start

  • Account list and the last closed month
  • Point-of-sale or billing exports if deposits are bundled
  • A note about Newbury Park versus the rest of the city, only if you want the books split that way
  • Your preparer's deadline

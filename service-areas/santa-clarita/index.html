How I can help

I offer bookkeeping support for businesses in Santa Clarita, including Valencia, Newhall, Saugus, and Canyon Country. Monthly bookkeeping, cleanup, and QuickBooks Online help can be scoped around your needs.

If the books are behind, tell me the last completed month and any accounts closed or replaced during the period. That helps establish a starting point before an ongoing monthly routine begins.

Monthly bookkeeping starts at $349. Cleanup and catch-up and QuickBooks Online support are quoted separately.

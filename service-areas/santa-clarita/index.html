Closing a Santa Clarita month

  1. Use the neighborhood name you use. Valencia, Newhall, Saugus, or Canyon Country. I still set the books up as your Santa Clarita business.
  2. List every account that touches the work. Including the card in the truck and the savings account that holds sales tax.
  3. Match the way you get paid. Insurance remits, card batches, progress invoices, or Shopify payouts.
  4. Categorize and reconcile. I code the month and tie the balances.
  5. Send what you asked the close to show. Profit and loss, balance sheet, and the comparison your plan includes.

Have this nearby

  • Last closed month, even if it was last year
  • Bank and card statements or QuickBooks feeds
  • Payroll reports from your payroll company, when you have staff
  • The lender's list, if a loan is the reason for the call

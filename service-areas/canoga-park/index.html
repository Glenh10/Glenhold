Bookkeeping in Canoga Park

Canoga Park is the older Valley neighborhood south of Chatsworth and north of Woodland Hills. Sherman Way and Topanga Canyon Boulevard are the working streets. Glenhold serves them from Simi Valley.

Book a Free Call

Glenhold Bookkeeping is a Simi Valley-based bookkeeping firm owned by Jack and operated by Glenhold Advisory LLC. Call (805) 419-3451 or email jack@glenhold.com. Hours are Monday through Saturday, 6am to 6pm Pacific. No street address is published. Day-to-day bookkeeping is remote, with in-person meetings by arrangement in the service area.

This is a dense grid of owner-run businesses: auto repair, trades, restaurants, markets, salons, and small professional offices. It is a poor place to bring an industry niche and a good place to bring a reconciliation habit. Glenhold's offer is the second one. If the books have been in a shoebox since last spring, that is a catch-up project, priced from the mess, not from the $349 monthly start.

The drive from Simi is often 25 to 40 minutes, down Topanga from the 118 or across from the west Valley. Close enough for a meeting, not close enough to invent a Canoga Park office. There isn't one. Winnetka, next door, does not have its own page. If that is where you are, you are still in the area Glenhold already describes. Say the neighborhood on the form.

Questions about Canoga Park

Is Winnetka covered too?

Winnetka sits between Canoga Park and Reseda and does not have a separate page. It is inside the same service area. Name it when you write so Jack knows where you are.

What if my Canoga Park books are a year behind?

That is catch-up work. The price depends on months, accounts, and missing statements. It is not the $349 monthly starting price. Monthly service can start after the backlog is done, if you want it.

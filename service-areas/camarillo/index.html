Bookkeeping for Camarillo small businesses

Camarillo bookkeeping from me is a closed month you can read. I categorize the transactions, reconcile your bank and credit cards, and send a profit and loss and a balance sheet, usually by the 15th. Old Town, the outlets, and the airport corridor are all in range. Plans start at $349.

Book a Free Call

Camarillo's work shows up in a few different places: Old Town along Ventura Boulevard, the outlet shops, the airport, and the agricultural businesses on the flats around the city. I keep books for all of them. The categories change with the business. The monthly promise stays the same.

An Old Town retailer needs the card batches explained. A shop at the outlets may have a sharp seasonal swing, and the quiet months still get a close so December is a month and February is a month. A business near the airport may have fuel, hangar or shop rent, and a handful of large invoices. An ag-adjacent owner may have seasonal receipts and equipment payments. I ask which of those is you, then I build the chart of accounts around the answer.

Tell me on the first call if the books are current. Current books go on a monthly plan. Open months are a catch-up, quoted from the records, and the plan starts when that stretch is done. I aim for reports on the 15th. You work with me. I've been in accounting since 2021, including audit work at PwC, and I'm a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor.

Sales tax you collect is a liability in the books, money on its way to the CDTFA. The statewide base rate is 7.25 percent. Camarillo's district rate can be higher, and I look up the one that applies to you. Your preparer files the return.

How a Camarillo month moves

  1. Place the business. Old Town, the outlets, the airport, or the fields at the edge of town. The address tells me which sales-tax district to check.
  2. Explain the deposit. Reader report, outlet batches, or invoices. I reconcile that detail to the bank.
  3. Name equipment and owner activity. Loan payments, vehicle costs, draws, and money you put back in.
  4. Categorize and reconcile. The month gets coded, then the bank and the cards tie out.
  5. Send the package. Profit and loss and balance sheet, aimed at the 15th, plus the extra reporting on Core and Strategic.

What I ask Camarillo owners to gather

  • Operating accounts and cards
  • The last month you trust
  • Processor or invoice detail behind the deposits
  • Equipment loans, if you want them on the balance sheet
  • Any date a lender or CPA has already set

Common questions

I run a shop in Old Town and the deposits come from a square reader and a few invoices. Can you reconcile both?

Yes. The reader report and the invoices sit next to the bank deposit. I want those three to agree.

Does a seasonal outlet shop still need a monthly close?

Yes, including the quiet months. A short month is still a month, and the year is easier when every month is closed.

How do you price a backlog?

From the months and the accounts you have. I quote that work before I start, separate from the monthly plan.

Can I stay on Essential if I only have one account?

Essential starts at $349 a month and is built for straightforward books. I confirm the price from your volume.

Who do I call with a question?

You call me. The close and the follow-up are with the same person.

Bookkeeping for San Fernando Valley small businesses

I keep the books for small businesses across the San Fernando Valley, from Chatsworth and Porter Ranch through the Boulevard neighborhoods to Burbank and Glendale. I categorize the month, reconcile your accounts, and send a profit and loss and a balance sheet, usually by the 15th. Plans start at $349. You work with me.

Chatsworth

Chatsworth, along Plummer, Mason, and De Soto, is the neighborhood closest to Simi Valley. Trades, warehouses, and owner-run shops are common. I categorize job costs, fuel, and materials, then reconcile the accounts you actually spend from.

Porter Ranch

Porter Ranch Town Center and the offices around it mix medical practices, services, and family businesses. Insurance deposits and card batches get matched to the report behind them, and owner draws stay visible.

Northridge

Northridge, around CSUN and the Reseda, Nordhoff, and Devonshire corridors, has shops and professional offices. I close the month from the processor report and the bank, including the weeks when the campus calendar changes how busy the door is.

Granada Hills

Granada Hills, along Balboa and Chatsworth Street, is home to practices, shops, and service firms. A straightforward account list often fits Essential. A practice that wants a quarterly view fits Core.

Canoga Park

Canoga Park runs along Sherman Way and Topanga. Shops and trades share the month with me when they want the deposit explained and the cards reconciled.

Reseda

Reseda Boulevard is a working street of services, shops, and small professional offices. I set the categories in the language you use at the counter, then I send the profit and loss and the balance sheet.

Woodland Hills

Woodland Hills covers Warner Center and Ventura Boulevard: larger offices, medical practices, and professional firms. Payroll stays with your payroll company. On Strategic I record the payroll journal entries. On every plan I label owner activity.

Encino

Encino along Ventura Boulevard is full of professional firms and medical offices. Retainers, insurance deposits, and rent are the usual puzzle. I reconcile them and send reports your CPA can use.

Tarzana

Tarzana sits on the Boulevard between Woodland Hills and Encino. I am glad to keep books for the practices, shops, and firms there. Bring the account list and the last month you trust.

Winnetka

Winnetka is a neighborhood of shops, trades, and home-service businesses. The close is the same personal one: categories that match the work, reconciled balances, and a report by the 15th.

Van Nuys

Van Nuys Boulevard, the civic center, and the airport area hold a wide mix of owner-run firms. Tell me which accounts are the business. I will quote the open months and start the rhythm from there.

Sherman Oaks

Sherman Oaks, along Ventura Boulevard and near Fashion Square, mixes professional services and shops. Card batches and invoices can live in one file if the categories keep them apart.

Studio City

Studio City along Ventura Boulevard has creative firms and professional offices. I record the month you had, including project deposits that belong to a later month, so the profit and loss stays honest.

North Hollywood

North Hollywood, around Lankershim and Magnolia and the Metro B and G lines, has shops, services, and firms that serve the neighborhood. I reconcile the accounts and send a month you can read.

Burbank

Burbank's downtown, Magnolia Park, and the area around Hollywood Burbank Airport are full of owner-run businesses. I keep their company books: sales, rent, payroll through your payroll company, and the monthly reports. Bring the accounts you use.

Glendale

Glendale, along Brand Boulevard, the Galleria, and the Americana, has professional offices, medical practices, and shops. I close the month and send the profit and loss and balance sheet, aimed at the 15th.

Common questions

Do you cover the whole Valley?

Yes. Chatsworth through Glendale, including the neighborhoods along Ventura Boulevard, are on this page.

I am in Winnetka or Tarzana. Is that included?

Yes. Both are part of this Valley work. Tell me the accounts and how you get paid.

Which plan should a small shop start with?

Essential starts at $349 a month for straightforward books. Core adds a quarterly view. Strategic adds custom reports and a monthly call.

Can you catch up a file that stopped last year?

Yes. I quote the open months from the records you have, then a monthly plan can carry the business forward.

Who files the tax return?

Your CPA files the return. I hand them clean, reconciled books.

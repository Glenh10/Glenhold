Bookkeeping on the Ventura County coast and Highway 126

I keep the books for owners in Port Hueneme, Santa Paula, Fillmore, and Ojai, and I am glad to talk with other towns along the coast and Highway 126. Ventura and Oxnard have their own pages. The close is monthly: categories, reconciled accounts, and reports, usually by the 15th.

Book a Free Call

Ventura and Oxnard are large enough, and different enough, to have their own pages. This page is for the towns around them: the harbor city of Port Hueneme, the Highway 126 cities of Santa Paula and Fillmore, Ojai up the 33, and the smaller places in between. I live in Simi Valley. The books happen in QuickBooks Online, and you work with me.

I've been in accounting since 2021, including audit work at PwC, and I'm a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor. I categorize the month, reconcile the accounts, and send a profit and loss and a balance sheet aimed at the 15th.

Port Hueneme

Port Hueneme is its own city, with the port and Naval Base Ventura County beside it. I keep books for the city's shops, trades, and harbor-side businesses. Tell me how you get paid and which accounts are the company's. If your work touches the base, bring the invoices and the accounts you already use. I record the activity that runs through your books.

Santa Paula

Santa Paula sits on Highway 126, with Main Street, a citrus history, and owner-run shops and crews that serve the valley. I set categories for a mix of counter sales, invoices, and seasonal receipts. Equipment payments and owner draws get named so the profit is visible. A backlog is quoted from the months you still have open.

Fillmore

Fillmore is the next city east on the 126. The businesses are local services, trades, food, and shops that know their customers by name. A monthly close here is often a short account list and a clear question: what did the month earn after fuel, materials, and the owner's draw? I answer that with a profit and loss and a balance sheet.

Ojai

Ojai is reached on Highway 33, with the Arcade, Libbey Park, and a year that swells in certain seasons. Shops, inns, restaurants, and studios all feel that calendar. I close every month, including the quiet ones, so the seasonal months can be read against a real baseline. Card batches and appointment deposits get reconciled to the report that explains them.

Other coast and 126 towns

Somis, Piru, Oak View, Meiners Oaks, and the other small communities along the coast and Highway 126 are welcome. Tell me the town, the accounts, and whether the books are current. I will quote the work and keep the same monthly rhythm.

Ventura, the county seat, is on the Ventura page. Oxnard, the largest city in the county, is on the Oxnard page.

What helps the first close

  • Seasonal notes, if some months are expected to look different
  • Processor reports when a deposit bundles sales, tips, and fees
  • Your CPA's deadline

Common questions

Do Ventura and Oxnard use this page?

They have their own pages. This page is for Port Hueneme, Santa Paula, Fillmore, Ojai, and the smaller towns nearby.

I am in Piru or Somis. Can I call?

Yes. If your business is on the coast or along the 126, I am glad to hear how you get paid and what you want the reports to show.

Ojai is seasonal. Do quiet months still close?

Yes. A quiet month is part of the year, and the comparison is clearer when it is closed too.

Can my CPA keep filing the return?

Yes. I hand your CPA clean, reconciled books. They file the return.

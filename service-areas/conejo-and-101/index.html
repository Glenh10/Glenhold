Bookkeeping along the Conejo and the 101

I keep the books for owners in Agoura Hills, Calabasas, Malibu, and Oak Park, and for the Thousand Oaks and Westlake Village businesses that sit on either side of them. The work is a monthly close: categorized transactions, reconciled accounts, and a profit and loss and balance sheet, usually by the 15th.

Book a Free Call

This page is for the communities between Thousand Oaks and the west San Fernando Valley: Agoura Hills, Calabasas, Malibu, and Oak Park. Thousand Oaks, including Newbury Park, and Westlake Village are full pages of their own because the work there is steady enough to deserve a closer description. If you are in those cities, start there. If you are in the towns below, stay here.

The monthly work is the same care I bring to Simi Valley. I've been in accounting since 2021, including audit work at PwC, and I'm a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor. I categorize the month around how you get paid, reconcile the bank and the cards, and send reports aimed at the 15th. You work with me.

Agoura Hills

Agoura Hills businesses cluster around Kanan Road and Reyes Adobe Road: professional offices, medical practices, restaurants, and owner-run shops. I want the deposit and the report behind it, whether that is a card batch or an invoice. Owner draws and the card that mixes personal spending get their own lines, so the profit and loss matches the practice or the shop you actually run. A current file becomes a monthly plan. Open months become a catch-up quote first.

Calabasas

Calabasas Road, The Commons, and the offices toward Malibu Canyon are full of professional firms, studios-of-one, and local services. The books I keep here often turn on a few large payments, rent, payroll through a payroll company, and a card for software and supplies. I label those so a quiet month and a busy month can be compared. Strategic adds a monthly call if you want to walk the numbers. Essential and Core send the reports in writing.

Malibu

Malibu owners call about shops, restaurants, design practices, and property-related businesses along the coast and up the canyon. I ask how money arrives and which accounts it lands in. Card batches, retainers, and deposits held until a job is finished each get a treatment that matches the timing. Sales tax you collect sits as a liability. The statewide base rate is 7.25 percent, and the district rate for your Malibu location can be higher. I look it up. Your preparer files the return.

Oak Park

Oak Park is an unincorporated community beside Thousand Oaks and Agoura Hills. I am glad to keep books for owners there: home-service crews, professional offices, and shops that serve the neighborhood. Tell me the accounts and the last month you trust. I will quote the open stretch if there is one, and the monthly close after that.

Thousand Oaks and Newbury Park are on the Thousand Oaks page. Westlake Village, a city in Los Angeles County, is on the Westlake Village page. If your week crosses those lines, say so on the first call and I will keep one set of books for the company.

What I need from a Conejo business

  • The town you work in, and the accounts in the business name
  • The last closed month
  • Processor or invoice detail when deposits are bundled
  • A CPA or lender date, if one is already on the calendar

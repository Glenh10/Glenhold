Glenhold Bookkeeping offers QuickBooks Online setup, consulting, and training for small business owners who want a clearer accounting system. Work directly with Jack, a Simi Valley local, former Big 4 accountant, and Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor.

This service is a fit if you want help setting up your books, improving an existing chart of accounts, learning a consistent bookkeeping routine, or checking the accuracy of work you handle yourself. Businesses across industries are welcome.

Start with the right structure

A useful QuickBooks file needs to reflect your business. Too many overlapping categories can make reports confusing; too few can hide the information you want to see. Setup begins with understanding how money moves through your business and which reports you need.

  • QuickBooks Online setup: establish a foundation for recording and reviewing your transactions.
  • Chart of accounts: organize income, expenses, assets, liabilities, and equity around your operations.
  • Bookkeeping workflow: discuss transaction review, reconciliation, and the information you should keep.
  • Accuracy check-ins: review your work on an agreed schedule and identify issues to address.

Learn how to maintain your books

Training can focus on the tasks you actually handle: reviewing bank activity, distinguishing transfers from income and expenses, organizing accounts, and understanding the difference between a profit and loss statement and a balance sheet.

Tell Jack which tasks are causing problems and what you want to do independently. The scope can be tailored to your current experience and your existing QuickBooks setup.

Already have an established QuickBooks file?

You do not have to start over to ask for help. Explain the current issue and whether the records are up to date. An existing file may need targeted consulting, a chart of accounts review, or a cleanup project before a regular routine makes sense.

Consulting or full monthly bookkeeping?

Choose consulting and training when you want to keep ownership of the day-to-day work and get help with setup or specific questions. Choose monthly bookkeeping when you want ongoing transaction categorization, reconciliation, and financial statements handled for you.

Consulting and training are scoped separately. The monthly bookkeeping plans start at $349 per month; that starting price is not a consulting or software subscription quote. Discuss your needs on a free introductory call.

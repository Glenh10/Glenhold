QuickBooks Online setup and training

A file that matches the business, and a routine you can actually follow.

Book a Free Call

I'll set QuickBooks up so it fits your business

If the file is noisy or you want to keep doing the entry yourself, I can set it up, train you, and check your work. I'm a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor and a former Big 4 accountant, in Simi Valley. I work in QuickBooks Online. If you're still on Desktop, tell me on the intro call before we start.

What setup covers

  • A QuickBooks Online file built around how money moves through the business.
  • A chart of accounts for income, expenses, assets, liabilities, and equity.
  • A routine for reviewing activity, separating transfers from income and expenses, and reconciling.
  • Accuracy check-ins on a schedule you agree to, if you want a review rather than a handoff.

Too many overlapping accounts make a profit and loss hard to read. Too few hide the thing you meant to see. Setup starts by asking which reports you actually use.

Training or a monthly plan

Choose training when you want to keep the day-to-day work. Choose monthly bookkeeping when you want me to categorize, reconcile, and send the statements. You can start with one and move to the other. I price training on its own. Monthly plans start at $349. You pay Intuit for the QuickBooks subscription.

An existing file does not have to be thrown out. It may need a chart review, or a cleanup, before a routine makes sense.

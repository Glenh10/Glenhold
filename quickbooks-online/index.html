QuickBooks Online help from a Certified ProAdvisor

Glenhold offers QuickBooks Online setup, consulting, and training for owners who want a clearer system or who want to keep doing the books themselves. Jack Hawley is a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor and a former Big 4 accountant, based in Simi Valley. The published offer is QuickBooks Online. QuickBooks Desktop is not a listed service. Say so on the intro call if your file is still on Desktop.

What setup covers

  • A QuickBooks Online file built around how money moves through the business.
  • A chart of accounts for income, expenses, assets, liabilities, and equity.
  • A routine for reviewing activity, separating transfers from income and expenses, and reconciling.
  • Accuracy check-ins on a schedule you agree to, if you want a review rather than a handoff.

Too many overlapping accounts make a profit and loss hard to read. Too few hide the thing you meant to see. Setup starts by asking which reports you actually use.

Training or a monthly plan

Choose training when you want to keep the day-to-day work. Choose monthly bookkeeping when you want categorization, reconciliation, and statements handled for you. You can start with one and move to the other. Consulting and training are scoped separately. The monthly plans start at $349. That number is not a training quote and it does not include the QuickBooks subscription, which you pay to Intuit.

An existing file does not have to be thrown out. It may need a chart review, or a cleanup, before a routine makes sense.

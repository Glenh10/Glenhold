Website privacy notice

Information about inquiries and website measurement.

Information you send

The inquiry form asks for your name, email address, optional phone number, the service you are interested in, and a message. Glenhold uses this information to respond to your inquiry and discuss services. Please do not send bank logins, account numbers, tax documents, or other sensitive financial records through this form.

Form submissions are processed through Formspree. Booking a call takes you to Google’s scheduling service. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers process information according to their own policies.

Website measurement

This website loads Google Ads and OpenAI Ads measurement tags to help measure visits and actions, including inquiry submissions and clicks on booking or telephone links. The website’s form-submission code does not include the name, email address, phone number, or message fields in the conversion events sent to those tags.

Third-party services may process technical information, such as browser, device, network, or advertising identifiers, under their own policies and applicable settings. This website also stores campaign parameters in browser session storage to help identify the source of an inquiry.

Questions about your information

Contact jack@glenhold.com with questions about this website’s information handling or requests concerning information you have provided.

Website forms and advertising measurement are separate from any arrangements for exchanging client bookkeeping records.

What actually drives the cost

I price from the work, not from a guess about your industry. These are the things that change the hours.

  • Transaction volume. A few dozen transactions a month is a different job from a few hundred.
  • Accounts to reconcile. Each bank account, credit card, and loan statement is another balance that has to tie out.
  • How money comes in. One deposit source is simpler than several payment processors or sales channels that have to be matched to the bank.
  • Payroll and loans. Pay runs, loan payments, and the principal-and-interest split take extra care.
  • The reports you need. A profit and loss statement and balance sheet are included. Job costing, classes, or accrual entries add scope.
  • The shape the file is in. A current QuickBooks file is monthly work. Months that were never finished are a separate cleanup quote.

What you get each month

On a monthly plan, I categorize income and expenses, reconcile the bank and credit card accounts in the scope, and send a profit and loss statement and a balance sheet. You can email me between closes when something doesn't look right.

I aim to deliver those reports by the 15th of the following month. That date holds once the statements are in and you have answered the questions that are blocking a number. If a receipt or a transfer is unclear, I would rather ask than guess.

Tax return preparation and filing aren't part of these plans. I can keep the books in a shape your tax preparer can use. Filing the return is their work, or yours.

Work that is quoted on its own

  • Cleanup and catch-up for months that are unfinished or no longer trustworthy.
  • QuickBooks Online setup and training if you want the file built, or you want to keep doing the books yourself.
  • Financial statements, CPA-ready books, and records for a financing request, when you need a specific package.

If the books are behind, don't force that history into a monthly price. We scope the catch-up first, then decide what “current” should cost going forward.

A fit for service businesses

Most of the quotes I write are for service businesses, contractors, home services, and professional services. A plumbing company, a consultant, and a home-service crew can land on different plans even when revenue looks similar, because the accounts and the job tracking are different.

If you're not sure which plan is closest, you don't need to decide it on this page. Tell me how you get paid and which accounts you use. I'll reply within one business day.

Common questions

Do you charge a flat monthly fee, or by the hour?

I charge a flat monthly fee for the scope we agree on. I don't bill the close by the hour, and I don't send an hourly invoice after the month is done. If the work changes, we reset the fee before it changes.

Is a QuickBooks Online subscription included?

No. The monthly fee is for the bookkeeping. Your QuickBooks Online subscription stays in your name and on your card. I need access to the file, and you keep the login.

How is catch-up or cleanup priced?

Cleanup is a project quote, separate from the monthly fee. I price it from how many months are unfinished, how many accounts are involved, and how much has to be corrected. A short gap on a simple file is a different job from a year of cards that were never reconciled.

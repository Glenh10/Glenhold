Cleanup and catch-up bookkeeping

I record the months that never got closed, reconcile those accounts, and send the reports. I quote the work from the records you have. A monthly plan can start when that stretch is finished.

Book a Free Call

Catch-up bookkeeping is the work of recording the months that never got closed. I categorize the backlog, reconcile the accounts for those months, and send you the reports. When the open stretch is finished, a monthly plan can keep the new months from stacking up again. You work with me. I quote the catch-up before I start.

I price that work from the records you have: how many months, how many accounts, and how complete the statements and reports are. A three-month gap with clean bank feeds is a different project from a year of mixed personal and business charges. I will tell you the price after I see the shape of the pile. I am glad to give you a range on the first call and a firm quote once the list of accounts is clear.

The first product of a catch-up is a starting point you can trust. That means a reconciled balance at the end of the last month in scope, a profit and loss for the period, and a balance sheet that ties. It also means a list of what I still could not see, so you and your CPA know the edges of the file. I hand your CPA clean, reconciled books. They file the return.

Bring what you have. Statements, processor exports, a half-used QuickBooks Online file, a spreadsheet, a box of PDFs. I can start from any of those. Months without a statement stay visible as a question, and I will tell you what that gap blocks. Plenty of catch-ups begin with 'the file stops in June' and end with a December that matches the bank.

After the backlog, most owners want the monthly rhythm. Essential, Core, and Strategic are the same plans as on a current file. Love your first month of books, or it's free, once that monthly work begins. The catch-up itself is quoted on its own. I've been in accounting since 2021, including audit work at PwC, and I'm a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor. Now I bring that same care to small businesses around Simi Valley.

How a catch-up actually moves

  1. You show me the last month you trust. That month is the floor. Everything after it is the project.
  2. I list the accounts and the gaps. Missing statements and missing months are named before I quote the final price.
  3. I categorize the open period. Using categories that match the business you have now, so the new monthly close can continue them.
  4. I reconcile each month or the period in the quote. Bank and cards tie at the end of the scope.
  5. You receive reports and a leftover list. Profit and loss, balance sheet, and the items that still need a human answer.
  6. The next month can be a plan. Monthly bookkeeping starts from the reconciled balance, on the plan that fits your volume.

What to send for a catch-up quote

  • The last month you believe is finished
  • Bank and card statements, or an invitation to the bank feeds
  • Any QuickBooks, spreadsheet, or processor export you already have
  • A note about personal spending on business accounts
  • The deadline, if a CPA or a lender is waiting

Common questions

How do you price a catch-up?

From the number of months, the accounts, and how complete the records are. I quote it before I start.

Can the monthly plan start immediately after?

Yes. The plan starts from the reconciled balance at the end of the catch-up.

What if a statement is missing?

I tell you which account it blocks. The rest of the period can still be categorized.

Will my CPA accept this?

I hand your CPA clean, reconciled books for the period in scope. They file the return.

Is there a standard timeline?

It follows the size of the pile and the deadline you have. I put the timing in the quote.
